Question paper (learners answer online and you watch live)

Paste all the questions, one per line. Put the marks at the end in brackets, like (5). Tap Build answer spaces, and each question gets its own answer box on the learner's screen. Live watching switches on automatically. If you leave this empty, learners upload a file instead.

Your teacher can watch this page live while you write. The portal also counts how many times you paste text or leave this tab. Nothing outside this page is recorded, and there is no camera or microphone.
